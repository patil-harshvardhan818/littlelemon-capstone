Our story

Little Lemon began with two brothers, one oven and a grandmother's recipe book. Every dish here is cooked the way it was cooked at the family table: simple ingredients, generous portions and a squeeze of citrus.

We buy our vegetables from farms around the city, bake our bread each morning and keep the menu short so everything on it is done well. The dining room is small, which is why we recommend booking ahead.

Book a table

Reservations are made through our booking API. It takes three requests:

  1. Create an account. POST /auth/users/ with a username and password.
  2. Get your token. POST /api-token-auth/ returns the token to send as Authorization: Token <your token>.
  3. Reserve. POST /restaurant/booking/tables/ with your name, number of guests and date.

You can only see and change your own bookings.
